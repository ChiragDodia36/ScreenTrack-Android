Privacy Policy

App: ScreenTrack  |  Last updated: April 9, 2026  |  Developer: Chirag Dodia

Your privacy matters. ScreenTrack does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for commercial purposes.

1. What ScreenTrack Does

ScreenTrack is a digital wellbeing application that periodically captures your device screen, analyses the content using artificial intelligence, and provides insights about your emotional state and screen usage patterns to help you build healthier digital habits.

2. Data We Collect

2a. Screen Content

With your explicit permission via Android's MediaProjection API, the app captures screenshots of your screen at regular intervals while the capture service is active.

  • Screenshots are sent to our secure backend server for AI analysis only.
  • Screenshots are not stored on our servers. They are processed in real-time and immediately discarded.
  • The resulting analysis data (emotion, sentiment score, content type, text snippet) is stored locally on your device only.

2b. Analysis Data (Stored Locally)

The following analysis results are saved to your device's local database:

  • Detected emotion (e.g. joy, neutral, sadness)
  • Sentiment score (a number between -1 and +1)
  • Content category (e.g. social media, news, work)
  • A short text snippet extracted from the screen
  • Timestamp of each capture

This data is stored for up to 30 days and is never uploaded or shared.

2c. Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash and error reports. This helps us identify and fix bugs. Crash reports may include:

  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • Stack trace at the time of the crash

No screen content or personal analysis data is included in crash reports.

2d. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage events such as when screen capture is started or stopped. This data is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify individual users from it.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Screen captures — processed server-side to generate emotion, sentiment, and content analysis. Discarded immediately after.
  • Local analysis data — displayed to you in the app as trends, insights, and wellness suggestions.
  • Crash reports — used solely to fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Analytics events — used to understand aggregate feature usage.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

The only third-party services used are:

  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics & Analytics) — governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • Groq API — used by the backend to run AI language models on the extracted text. Governed by Groq's Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All analysis data is stored only on your device using Android's Room database.
  • Communication between the app and backend uses HTTPS encryption.
  • The backend API is protected with an API key — only the official app can submit data.
  • Local data is retained for 30 days by default, after which it is automatically deleted.

6. Your Rights & Controls

  • Stop capture at any time — tap Stop on the Home screen. No data is collected when the service is off.
  • Delete all data — go to Settings → Data Management → Clear Data to permanently delete all locally stored analysis data.
  • Uninstall — uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

ScreenTrack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
